--- DESCRIPTION ---
WinrSwipe is a local marketplace for Israel. Swipe through listings, place a bid, and message the seller to arrange the handoff.

HOW IT WORKS
• Swipe through live listings
• Place a bid, or open a listing to message the seller
• Publish an item with photos from the Publish tab
• Chat inside the app to agree on the handoff

ACCOUNT
• Sign in with email and password, or with Sign in with Apple
• Phone number is optional
• Delete your account anytime from the Profile tab

WinrSwipe is free to use.

Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are available in the app and on the web.

Notes (English, paste into App Review Information → Notes):

This build is the free WinrSwipe marketplace. There is no checkout and no in-app purchase.

1. Sign in with the demo email and password on this page, or with Sign in with Apple.
2. After sign-in the home feed lists active items. The default filter is all of Israel. Do not expect the feed to filter to the review device's city.
3. Swipe a card, place a bid, or open a listing and tap the contact button to message the seller in chat.
4. The Publish tab creates a listing that appears in the feed immediately.
5. Account deletion: Profile tab → Delete account → confirm. This permanently deletes that account on the server and signs out. Please use a throwaway account if you test deletion; the demo account above should stay available for sign-in.

Phone number on registration is optional. Location permission is requested only if the reviewer taps the nearby filter.
